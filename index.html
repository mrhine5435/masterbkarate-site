FAQs

What should I wear to my first class?

Comfortable athletic clothing. We'll size your uniform if you enroll.

Do I need prior experience?

No experience needed — we have beginner-friendly options for all ages.

Do you offer family discounts?

Yes — ask about multi-student and parent-child pricing.

Is sparring required?

Never for beginners. It's optional and safely supervised when you're ready.
